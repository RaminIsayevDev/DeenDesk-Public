# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 13, 2026**

I, **Ramin Isayev** (operating as “Ramin Labs”), am the developer of the **DeenDesk** mobile application (the “App”). This page informs you of my policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use my App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**DeenDesk** is designed as a focused application for displaying religious texts, providing a spiritual clock interface, and offering prayer-related utilities.

- **Personal Data:** I do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise real-time location.
- **Approximate Location Data:** If you enable the automatic location mode, the App accesses your device's approximate location (Coarse Location). This data is processed locally on your device solely to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to my servers, stored, or shared by me with third parties.
- **Local Storage:** All user preferences (such as theme, language settings, and manually selected cities) are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore and are not accessible by me.

## 2. Third-Party Services and APIs

While I do not collect your data directly, the App integrates third-party services and APIs for core functionality.

**A. Google Play Services:**

The App uses services provided by Google, which may collect certain information, such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and crash reports, for diagnostics, security, and service functionality.

- **Google Play Services:** Used for core app functionality.
- **Google Play Integrity API:** Used to help ensure the security and integrity of the App.

**B. External API Connections:**

To provide religious data and related functionality, the App connects to the following external APIs. No personal identifiers are intentionally sent by the App. However, these servers may temporarily process your IP address to complete network requests.

- **Aladhan API:** Used to synchronize Hijri calendar dates and verify prayer times.
- **Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) API:** Used in manual search mode to convert your manually entered city name into geographical coordinates.
- **Audio Hosting Providers:** Used to stream Quran audio recitations for the built-in media player.

## 3. Permissions

To function properly, the App may request the following device permissions:

- **Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):** Required to automatically calculate prayer times and Qibla direction based on your approximate location. You can refuse this permission and use manual city search instead.
- **Internet Access (INTERNET):** Required to stream Quran audio, synchronize the Hijri calendar, and search for cities in manual mode.
- **Notifications:** Used to deliver prayer time alerts, if enabled by the user.

The App does not request permissions to access your camera, microphone, contacts, or personal files.

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App does not require user accounts or directly collect personal information, it can be used by audiences of all ages.

## 5. Hardware and Screen Usage Disclaimer

The App is designed to keep the screen active for extended periods to display religious content and clock interfaces.

- **OLED/AMOLED Screens:** While the App includes an "OLED Screen Protection" feature to minimize risks, prolonged display of static elements on OLED/AMOLED screens carries an inherent risk of permanent image retention ("burn-in").
- **Limitation of Liability:** The Developer provides the App "as is" and shall not be held liable for hardware damage, including but not limited to screen burn-in, pixel degradation, or accelerated battery wear resulting from the use of the App. Users are responsible for the usage and maintenance of their device's hardware.

## 6. Data Deletion

Since the App does not require user accounts and I do not store users' personal information on external servers, there is no personal account data maintained by me that requires deletion.

- **Local Data:** You can delete all your local preferences and settings at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
